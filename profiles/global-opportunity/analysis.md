\# Evaluation goal



Evaluate whether this item reveals an important global change or a potentially actionable business opportunity.



The reader wants to understand the world, discover emerging trends, identify unmet demand, and find realistic entrepreneurial or commercial opportunities.



\# Scoring rubric



\*\*9-10: Major opportunity signal\*\*

A structural change, major policy shift, rapidly emerging market, severe supply-demand mismatch, disruptive technology, major consumer behavior change, or new business model with clear commercial consequences.



\*\*7-8: Strong signal\*\*

A credible development that could create meaningful demand, reduce costs, change an industry, open a new market, or create opportunities for businesses and entrepreneurs.



\*\*5-6: Worth monitoring\*\*

Interesting trend or early signal, but commercial significance, timing, or demand is still uncertain.



\*\*3-4: Weak signal\*\*

Useful information but limited commercial implications, small market impact, or mostly incremental development.



\*\*0-2: Noise\*\*

Routine technical updates, unsupported speculation, marketing hype, minor product releases, generic commentary, or content without a plausible real-world opportunity.



\# Evaluation guidance



Judge the item on:



1\. Magnitude — how large could the change become?

2\. Demand — does it create or reveal a real customer problem?

3\. Willingness to pay — who could realistically spend money solving it?

4\. Timing — is the opportunity emerging now or already overcrowded?

5\. Accessibility — could a small company, student, freelancer, or startup participate?

6\. Transferability — could a proven model in China, the US, Europe, Japan, India, Southeast Asia or the Middle East be applied elsewhere?

7\. Evidence — distinguish verified facts from forecasts and hype.

8\. Durability — is this structural or merely a temporary news spike?



Prefer concrete numbers, adoption data, policy changes, customer behavior, supply constraints and business activity.



Use three to five specific topic tags.
