# \# Global opportunity radar

# 

# Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding a meaningful global change that may create business, investment, entrepreneurial, industrial, consumer, or strategic opportunities.

# 

# Relevant topics include:

# \- major global events, geopolitics, wars, sanctions, diplomacy and policy changes

# \- macroeconomics, interest rates, inflation, employment, trade and supply chains

# \- companies, financing, M\&A, IPOs, bankruptcies and major strategic shifts

# \- AI, robotics, automation, semiconductors, energy, biotech and emerging technologies

# \- consumer behavior, demographics, migration, education and employment changes

# \- shortages, falling costs, new regulations, subsidies and industry bottlenecks

# \- rapidly growing products, services, markets and business models

# \- developments that reveal unmet demand or a new willingness to pay

# \- business models proven in one country that may be transferable to another

# 

# Prefer changes with real-world consequences over routine commentary.

# 

# Do not route routine software releases, programming tutorials, minor open-source updates, generic opinion pieces, memes, or technical discussions here unless they reveal a significant new market, customer need, cost reduction, regulatory shift, or commercial opportunity.

# 

# The profile should favor signal over hype.
